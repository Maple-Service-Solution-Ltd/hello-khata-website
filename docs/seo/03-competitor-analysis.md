# 03 · Competitor analysis

Sources: web searches run on 2026-11-24. Almost all claims below are **the vendors' own marketing**, unverified. User counts and prices are self-reported or come from marketplace listings. No ranking positions are given, because none were measured.

Regulatory context: MSH reports that pharmacy management software is a prerequisite for DGDA "Model Pharmacy" status. That is a possible content angle, but **verify the current DGDA requirements before writing about them**.

HelloKhata angle: prescription reading (OCR), plus batch and expiry tracking (both marked available in `capabilities.ts`), in a cloud app with Bangla UI and voice.

## Segment 3 — Super shop / retail ERP

KarbariPlus (Ambala IT), Managerium Retail ERP, PrismERP, DataSoft POS, MySoftHeaven, HisabPlus, plus many BDStall "super shop POS + hardware" packages at roughly ৳10k–60k. Shopify POS also has Bangla-language multi-store POS pages on shopify.com/bd.

Signal: listicles like "top 10 POS software in Bangladesh 2026" (ibos.io) and vendor "best software company for super shop" pages occupy the SERP.

HelloKhata angle: a transparent monthly price on `/pricing`, instead of the one-off licence plus hardware packages common in this segment.

## Segment 4 — Online store builders (HelloKhata: planned)

DeshiCommerce (RedX/Pathao courier, bKash merchant), Bitcommerz (Pathao, Steadfast, RedX, Paperfly), BDBONIK (bKash, COD, 7-day trial), Shopify plus local courier apps, and WooCommerce plugins (BanglaPress). The segment is mature and crowded. **Do not invest SEO here until the product ships.**

## Segment 5 — F-commerce tools (HelloKhata: planned)

**Pathao Commerce** (Sep 2026: unified Messenger/Instagram/WhatsApp inbox via official Meta integrations), Selorax, FCommerceOS (youthfireit), ShopZero, Karbari. Pathao entering this segment raises the bar. **Do not invest SEO here until the product ships.**

## Takeaways

1. **The brand has no search footprint yet.** Competitors have press coverage, app listings and blogs. The fastest gains are:
   - Search Console;
   - accurate titles (done);
   - app store listings;
   - the existing Facebook and LinkedIn profiles cross-linking to the site.
2. **The defensible gaps** are:
   - a Bangla voice/AI assistant answering from your own records;
   - pharmacy prescription reading;
   - one product spanning grocery → pharmacy → super shop.

   Few competitor pages target these terms (qualitative observation, not measured).
3. **Pricing transparency** is a differentiator against "call for price" and hardware-package listings. Keep `/pricing` crawlable and specific.
4. **No comparison pages that name competitors** until claims about them can be verified and kept current. Comparison content goes stale fast and carries legal risk.
